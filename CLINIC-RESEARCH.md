## How ClinicFlow AI fits (observations → questions, not claims)

1. **"Website form → WhatsApp" means each enquiry lives in a chat thread.** **ASK:** how do you track which enquiries are pending, who replied, and who never converted? → *Fit: the Care Desk inbox + pipeline gives one shared view across both branches.*
2. **Repeat admin questions (hours, location, how to book, rescheduling) consume staff time.** **ASK:** roughly how many of these per day? → *Fit: approved-answers assistant replies only admin-approved text; anything clinical/price goes to staff.*
3. **Two branches, shared services.** **ASK:** does each branch handle its own enquiries? → *Fit: rule-based assignment (e.g. treatment coordinator for braces/aligners/implants).*
4. **No visible follow-up mechanism** for enquiries that don't convert. **ASK:** do staff chase unanswered enquiries? → *Fit: consent-based follow-ups with automatic suppression (opt-out, takeover, confirmed).*
5. **Hiring + growth** (10+ dentists, medical tourism) suggests volume growth → *Fit: audit trail, analytics by source (Website/WhatsApp/Facebook/Walk-in), CSV export without patient-identifying data.*

Pitch framing: **not "your process is broken"** — "you've built impressive demand through WhatsApp and reviews; the Care Desk gives your team one organised workspace around it."
